# Methodology — news-image-20261118

## How the news was chosen

On 19 November 2026 (after 18 November had ended everywhere on Earth) I searched the
day's reporting for Wednesday, 18 November 2026 and picked the six most consequential
stories, spread across regions and fields: diplomacy (Asia), war (Europe), the Gulf
(Middle East), US politics (North America), tech/business, and weather (South Asia).
Two further stories went into the "Also in brief" strip. Selection rule: an event had to
have happened, or been first reported, on 18 November 2026 — nothing from before
13 November 2026 appears. Each story was checked against at least two independent
sources before it was used; the full list is in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

What was left out on purpose: the trilateral Trump–Xi–Putin summit itself (it happens
on Thursday 19 November, so only the Wednesday arrivals and the Xi–Putin meeting are
shown), and the cyclone's landfall (forecast for Friday 20 November — only Wednesday's
evacuation orders are shown). No speculation is shown as fact: casualty and damage
figures are the ones the cited outlets reported.

## How the image was made

The poster was drawn programmatically with Python (Pillow), not generated by an AI
image model, so every word is spelled exactly as written and stays sharp. Layout:
a newspaper masthead, a full-width navy date band reading "WEDNESDAY, 18 NOVEMBER
2026", six story cards (kicker, headline, one-line summary) in a 2×3 grid, an
"Also in brief" strip, and a source line. Fonts are Liberation Serif (headlines) and
Liberation Sans (body), both open-licensed. Text is wrapped by measured pixel width so
nothing overflows its card. The finished PNG was read back and inspected visually:
all text legible, no clipping, no garbled characters.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the checked `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). A file copy is byte-for-byte
deterministic: two runs write identical bytes. The entry runs with no network.
